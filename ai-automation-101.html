Three paths
How people actually make money with AI automation
Path 01 — Selling

Agency Owner / Sales Rep

This is where the real money is. You do outreach, talk to business owners, identify their problems, and sell the automation solution. As an agency owner you run the whole operation. As a sales rep you close deals for someone else's agency and earn 20–30%+ commission on every sale. No code required — just sales skills and hustle.

Path 02 — Developing

Backend Developer / Builder

You're behind the scenes building the actual automations. Agency owners send you the client brief and you build it. No sales calls, no client interaction — just building with tools like n8n, VAPI, and Claude. You can work with multiple agencies at once and get paid per build or on retainer.

Path 03 — Full Stack Founder

Solo Founder Who Does Both

You sell and you build — or you sell and hire developers to build. This is the agency owner model. Most successful founders start solo, close their first few clients, then hire developers on the back end so they can focus purely on sales and growth.

What nobody tells you
The thing I wish someone told me straight
01

It's all outreach.

Once you have a solid outreach system and you know how to sell, everything else falls into place. The product is easy. Getting in front of people consistently is the real work.

02

Don't try to do everything yourself.

The biggest mistake new agency owners make is trying to sell AND build AND manage clients all at once. Use leverage. Hire developers on the back end. Your job is to sell.

03

You don't need to know how to code to start.

You need to understand AI automation well enough to sell it and manage the process. The technical build can be outsourced. Sales cannot be outsourced when you're starting out.

04

The minimum viable agency is simple.

You, an outreach system, one or two core products, and a developer on the back end. That's it. You don't need a big team or a big budget to get your first client.
