System 04 — Convert More Estimates
The Estimate Follow-Up Bot

Someone gets a quote and doesn't book. Right now that lead just disappears. This bot follows up automatically — at the exact right time — and captures the jobs that would otherwise go to whoever calls them next.

How it works
01

Quote Sent — No Booking

Customer got a price via text, web form, or voice agent but didn't confirm. Timer starts automatically.

02

Follow-Up Fires in 2–4 Hours

"Still thinking about it? We can get there same-day if you're ready." Short, direct, zero pressure.

03

Second Follow-Up Next Morning

If still no response — one final message the next morning. After that, the system respects the silence.

04

Booking Link Always Included

Every follow-up has a one-tap booking link. Friction removed. Decision is easy.

If GoJunk sends 40 quotes a week and only 10% of the unbooked ones respond to a follow-up — that's 4 extra jobs a week at an average of $200 each. $800/week in recovered revenue. $41,600/year. Fully automated.
