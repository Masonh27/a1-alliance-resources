Step 1 — Your Niche

You're in the AI automation space. That's your niche. Now get specific — are you selling voice agents, workflow automations, or full backend systems? Pick one to lead with. The riches are in the niches.

🏷️

Step 2 — Your Business Name

Create a name for your agency. Keep it clean, professional, and easy to remember. This is the brand you'll be building everything around — don't overthink it, just pick one and move.

🌐

Step 3 — Build a Website

Use Framer.com — it's free. This doesn't need to be perfect. Think of it as a business card. If a prospect asks if you have a website, you hand them this. Add any social proof you have. If you have none, just make a clean page about your agency.

Your Elevator Pitch

"I run an AI automation agency where we develop workflows and backend systems for businesses to help them operate smoothly without losing revenue and time."

Memorize this. Use it everywhere.

Step 4
Set up Apify and start pulling leads

Apify is completely free to use and it's how you build your cold outreach list without spending a dollar. Use the Google Lead Scraper to pull businesses in whatever category you're targeting.

1️⃣

Create Your Apify Account

Go to apify.com and sign up for free. No credit card required to start scraping leads.

2️⃣

Use the Google Lead Scraper

Search for the business types you want to target. Pull their name, phone number, website, and location — everything you need to start calling.

3️⃣

Export to Google Sheets

Export your lead list into Google Sheets. This becomes your tracking system for every call, follow-up, and outcome.

Best targets to start with: HVAC companies, plumbers, dental offices, law firms, real estate agencies, auto detailers. These businesses have repetitive workflows and the budget to fix them.

Step 5
Get into every space possible
🔵 LinkedIn — Your #1 Tool
→ Set up a professional profile that clearly says what you do
→ Post 2–3 times per day about AI automation, your journey, results
→ Send connection requests to business owners daily
→ DM leads with a pain-point focused message — not a pitch
🌐 Join Communities
→ Facebook groups in your target industries
→ Local business networking events
→ Online entrepreneur communities and Discord servers
→ Anywhere your potential clients spend time

Don't pitch immediately. Lead with the pain point. Nobody cares what tool you use — they care that their problem gets solved.

The real work
Cold calling is the engine. There's no way around it.

If you're running a lean business with little upfront capital, cold calling is your highest ROI activity. It costs nothing and it works. Be ready to do a lot of it.

📞 How to Structure the Call
1️⃣

Lead with their pain point

Don't open with "I sell AI automation." Open with "I noticed most businesses in your space struggle with X — is that something you deal with?"

2️⃣

Make it about their problem

The solution doesn't matter yet. Get them talking about the bottleneck. Let them sell themselves on needing help.

3️⃣

Get them on a discovery call

The goal of the cold call is ONE thing — book the next call. That's it. Don't try to close on a cold call.

⚠ Mindset Check

Most people quit before they get their first yes. The people who win are the ones who make the most calls. Volume is the strategy when you're starting out. Learn sales. Practice sales. Get good at sales.

📚 Invest in Sales Skills

Study Jeremy Miner, Alex Hormozi, and Brian Tracy. Sales is the single most important skill in this business. The technical stuff can be outsourced. Sales cannot.

How to Price Your Services
Mason's Pricing Rule

Charge at minimum 100% more than what it costs to run. Ideally 3–4x. Factor in API costs, webhook costs, your labor, and your time.

Cost to run the automation $25/mo example
Minimum charge (2x) $50/mo
Ideal charge (3–4x) $75–$100/mo
Starting out? No social proof? Start cheap. Grow 30%

Start at a lower rate to land your first clients and build case studies. Increase your price by 30% as your results and reputation grow.

Getting Paid
Set up Stripe before your first call
Why Stripe

Stripe is completely free to set up. When a prospect says "let's do it" on a call, you need to be able to send them a payment link immediately. Don't lose momentum waiting to get your payments sorted.

Best First Product to Sell

Voice Agents. They're in high demand right now, businesses understand the concept quickly, and they're one of the easier automations to explain on a cold call. Lead with voice agents.

Steps 6 & 7
Set your daily minimums and track everything
📊 Daily Minimums

Set non-negotiable daily numbers and hit them every day regardless of how you feel. This is what separates people who get clients from people who just talk about getting clients.

Cold calls per day Minimum 20–50
LinkedIn connections sent 10–20 per day
LinkedIn posts 2–3 per day
DMs / follow-ups As many as possible
📋 Track Everything in Google Sheets

Create a simple spreadsheet to track every outreach action. This is how you find what's working and cut what isn't.

→ Business name, contact info, industry
→ Date contacted, method used, outcome
→ Follow-up date and next steps
→ Notes from the conversation

Build off what works. Cut what doesn't. Review your sheet weekly and double down on the outreach channels that are generating responses.
